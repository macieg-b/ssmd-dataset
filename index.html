SSMD – SMS Stealing Malware Dataset

The SSMD (SMS Stealing Malware Dataset) is a curated collection of Android malware samples designed for research on SMS interception and OTP-related threats.

Access to the dataset is granted for academic and research purposes upon request. Please complete the request form below. After verification and approval, the dataset (package names and SHA-256 hashes in CSV format) will be provided via e-mail.
